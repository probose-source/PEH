PRECISION POINT · No. 014

The spooky side may be wiring.

30 horses · 1 umbrella

A person opened an umbrella about 16 ft from each horse and walked toward it, on three test days. The horses moved farthest when the approach came from the left (Laterality, 2007).

Right side first: no gap

Horses that met the umbrella on the right side first showed no left-right difference, which the authors read as learning crossing from one side of the brain to the other. A second lab (Laterality, 2006; 65 young horses) found that the more emotional the horse, the more likely it looked at a new object with its left eye.

This week at the barn:
A two-week spook log: every spook or hard look, left or right, what it was, new or familiar.
If one side wins, ask your trainer which side meets new things first in a new ring.
A sudden change in how your horse reacts goes to your veterinarian.

Right side first + a calm look = less left-side drama (in the study).
