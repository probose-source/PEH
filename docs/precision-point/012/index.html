PRECISION POINT · No. 012

A sprinkle of chaff doesn’t slow the bucket.

15% → same speed

In a feeding study of 6 horses and 10 meal types, the barn’s usual mix with 15% chaff was eaten as fast as plain pellets (Pferdeheilkunde, 2005). A handful on top of the grain is decoration, not a brake.

~6 → ~12½ min

a 2-lb meal: pellets alone vs. pellets with 20% chopped straw mixed through, about twice as long. Long or short chop (about 1 in vs 1.6 in) made no difference, and a second study in oats found eating slowed significantly only above 15% chaff (Journal of Equine Veterinary Science, 2019).

This week at the barn:
Have your groom weigh tonight’s grain and the chaff and write both numbers down (chaff ÷ total = your percentage).
Time the meal from bucket in to licked clean, three nights in a row.
Then change only the chaff, and time three more nights.

A handful + the same grain = the same speed. About 20% chopped forage = about twice the time. Chaff works by the pound, not by the handful.
