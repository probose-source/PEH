Why do white pasterns get scratches easily?

Clover + sun + pink skin

Some pasture plants (St. John’s wort, buckwheat, some clovers) put light-sensitive compounds into the skin. Dark skin has melanin, a built-in sunscreen; the pink skin under white socks doesn’t.

In a Swiss study of 50 young horses checked weekly from August to October, white pasterns were affected significantly more often than dark ones, and moisture was the other big trigger. Same horse, same field, only the white legs flare? That’s a clue. Walk the paddocks with your barn manager, photograph white and dark legs side by side every week, and bring the pattern to your vet. Many causes look alike on the lower leg.
