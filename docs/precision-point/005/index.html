Influenza is a virus your horse catches and clears. EHV-1 is a herpesvirus most horses already carry for life, reactivated by transport and hard work. The flu half of the spring syringe does nothing for EHV-1. The rhino half does real work — shedding and abortion — and stops short of EHM, the form behind last winter’s multi-state outbreak.
