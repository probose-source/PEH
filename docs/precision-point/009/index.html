for the rest of the field (median 4 of 24). More signs went with lower dressage marks. The researchers treat 8 or more (a third) as the point to bring in your veterinarian.

Four groups, easier than 24:
Face (12): ears pinned, mouth open, head tilted
Tail (2): clamped or swishing
Stride (6): rushing, toe dragging, breaking gait
Reactions (4): spooking, stopping, bucking

A sign is a signal, not misbehavior. Ride signs + feed + feet + movement = the whole horse. Film a normal ride from the side and count, then read it beside how your horse is fed, shod and moving.
