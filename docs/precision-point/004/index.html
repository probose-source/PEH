Stop patting. Start scratching.

heart rate when horses were groomed at the withers, the spot they groom each other — about twice the drop from grooming the mane (Animal Behaviour, 1993; repeated by three later studies).

Watch two horses in a field. They nuzzle. They groom each other. Nobody pats anybody. A pat says thank you in your language; a scratch says it in the horse’s. After the round: a slow scratch at the base of the mane, and watch the head drop and the eye soften.
